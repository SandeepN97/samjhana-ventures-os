Components

Navigation

AdminBottomNav

The bar fixed to the bottom of the screen with four places: Home, Records, Analytics and Settings.

AdminBusinessTabs

The row of pill tabs along the bottom of a business's header: Overview, Products, New sale, Orders, (Customers), Online orders and Website page.

AdminLanguageToggle

The pill that switches the admin between Nepali and English.

AdminPageHeader

The coloured bar at the top of every admin screen: a back button, an optional icon, the title, an optional subtitle, optional actions and the language toggle.

WebFooter

The dark footer of the public website: name and short blurb, three link columns (Shop, Visit, Contact) and a bottom line with the copyright and a closing note.

WebNavbar

The top bar of the public website: the grid mark and name on the left, four links, language label, call, WhatsApp and directions icons, the cart and a dark "Talk to us" button.

Actions

AdminButton

The standard admin button: bold, rounded-xl and never shorter than 44 px.

WebButtons

The three button styles of the public website, defined once in samjhana-web/src/index.css: btn-dark, btn-outline and btn-gold.

WebPills

Rounded filter pills and the small uppercase label that opens a section.

Containers

AdminCard

The standard white content card: rounded-xl bg-white p-4 shadow-sm.

Forms

AdminFormField

A label above a field.

Status

AdminStatusBadge

A small rounded pill that says what state something is in.

WebBadgePill

A tiny uppercase badge on a beekeeping product: "Raw", "New", "Best seller" and so on.

Feedback

AdminConfirmDialog

A question in a pop-up in the middle of the screen, used before anything that cannot be undone.

AdminToast

A short message that appears near the bottom of the screen after something happened, then goes away by itself: success (green), error (red) or info (blue).

Dialogs

WebStoryModal

The short "story" pop-up opened from a WebProductTile: a small label, a heading, a paragraph and one dark button, on a honey-yellow card.

Cards

WebBeeProductCard

A product card on the beekeeping page: picture area, optional badge, name, Nepali name, short description, price and a small cart button.

WebHubLinkCard

A small white card with an icon, a name and its Nepali name, used in the two big panels at the top of the home page: "Visit" (services) and "Shop".

WebProductTile

The "From our shop" tiles on the home page: a soft green picture area with the product's picture (or a shopping-bag icon when there is none), the name and its Nepali name.

Sections

WebTrustStrip

The row of numbers under the home page's two big panels: "16+ years in Gulmi", "4 ways to serve you", "2008 established".

Screens

AdminHomeScreen

The "Dad-proof" home screen: the name mark, today's cash, one big tile per business, a few wide buttons and the bottom bar.

Brand

AdminWordmark

The name mark of the admin: a building icon and company name, shown on the login screen, the home screen, and the expanded desktop sidebar.
